      • canConnect

        public boolean canConnect(Hash from, Hash to)
        Can "from" connect to "to" based on published addresses?

        This is intended for tunnel candidates, where we already have the RI. Will not force RI lookups. Either from or to may be us.

        This is best effort, as we can't know for sure. Published addresses or introducers may have changed. Even if a can't connect to b, they may already be connected as b connected to a.

        Parameters:
        from - the source router hash
        to - the destination router hash
        Returns:
        true if we don't have either RI
