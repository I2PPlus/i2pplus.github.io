      • getBanNextHopChecked

        public String getBanNextHopChecked()
        Get the checked status of the next-hop ban option.

        When enabled (default), peers resolved from the network that we are not directly connected to - transit next hops as well as client tunnel endpoints - are subject to policy bans like any other peer. When disabled, such peers are exempt from policy bans unless we are directly connected to them.

        Returns:
        "checked" if enabled, empty string otherwise
