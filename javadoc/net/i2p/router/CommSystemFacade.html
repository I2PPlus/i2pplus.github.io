      • wasFailsafeClosed

        public abstract boolean wasFailsafeClosed(Hash peer, long withinMs)
        Whether any transport closed a session to the given peer for idleness within the given window.

        True means this router declined to keep talking to the peer, which is the opposite of the peer having gone away. Callers attribute a later loss of connectivity with it, and must treat a false as "no information" rather than as evidence the peer is at fault: only a transport that remembers the close can answer, and an answer of false covers never-established, still-open, and closed-for-other-reasons alike.

        Parameters:
        peer - the peer to ask about, non-null
        withinMs - how far back counts as recent in ms; non-positive means nothing is recent
        Returns:
        true when some transport closed a session to the peer that recently
