    public class IterativeSearchJobextends FloodSearchJob
    Traditional Kademlia iterative search with enhanced robustness.

    Continues searching when initial lookup fails by iteratively querying closer-to-the-key peers returned in DatabaseSearchReplyMessages. Unlike traditional Kademlia, doesn't stop when no closer keys are available - keeps searching until timeout or maximum search limit is reached.

    Key differences from FloodOnlySearchJob: - Chases peers in DSRM immediately rather than waiting - Searches one peer at a time with individual per-search timeouts - Uses total timeout rather than per-search timeouts - Longer overall timeout for better success rates - Reduces search traffic by half compared to parallel approaches

    Advantages over flood-only approaches: - More robust in large networks with incomplete floodfill knowledge - Better success rates through sequential searching - Reduced network traffic through single-peer queries.

      • _maxRouterInfoLookupTime

        private static volatile int _maxRouterInfoLookupTime
        Adaptive deadline cap (ms) for RouterInfo lookups. Used for transit next-hop lookups, where a message is held until the lookup completes or times out; a long hold during a spike tanks transit throughput. Defaults to a shorter cap than the max search time so a missing or unreachable RouterInfo fails fast and the source can reroute. Tuned live by the Tuner toward ~4x the observed netDb.successTime.

      • widenCandidatesLocked

        private void widenCandidatesLocked()
        When fewer than BETA_CONFIRM peers have been queried and the candidate list is exhausted, widen the candidate set once with the closest known non-floodfill peers, so a failure is confirmed against beta closest peers rather than declared after one or two misses. One-shot: a search widens at most once. Caller must hold this lock.

      • addClosest

        static void addClosest(Collection<Hash> candidates, Collection<Hash> allRouters, Hash rkey, Hash us, Hash key, int cap)
        Add the closest cap peers of allRouters, by XOR distance from rkey, excluding us and key. Package private for testing.

        Selects the cap closest rather than sorting everything: allRouters is the whole netdb (up to 10 000 hashes) and this runs on the first failed lookup of a key, so a full O(n log n) sort bought an ordering of entries nobody reads. A bounded farthest-first heap keeps the cap closest in O(n log cap), and us and the target are skipped while walking instead of by two O(n) list removals afterwards.

        Parameters:
        candidates - the search's candidate set to add to
        allRouters - known router hashes
        rkey - the routing key the search is chasing
        us - our router hash
        key - the lookup target
        cap - max peers to add; nothing is added when it is not positive
