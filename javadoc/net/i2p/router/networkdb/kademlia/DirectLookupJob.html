    Job
    class DirectLookupJobextends FloodOnlySearchJob
    Ask a connected peer for his RI. Modified from SingleSearchJob.

    Mainly for older routers. As of 0.9.55, transports will periodically send their RI. Some old routers may not respond or may send DSRM, e.g. if hidden (and i2pd?).
