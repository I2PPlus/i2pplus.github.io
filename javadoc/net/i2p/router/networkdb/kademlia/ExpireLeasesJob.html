    Job
    class ExpireLeasesJobextends JobImpl
    Periodically searches through all leases to find expired ones, failing those keys and firing up a new search for each (in case we want it later, might as well preemptively fetch it). Also refreshes leasesets that are about to expire to ensure continuity of service.
