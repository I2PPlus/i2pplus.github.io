    Enclosing class:
    RatchetPayload
    public static interface RatchetPayload.PayloadCallback
    For all callbacks, recommend throwing exceptions only from the handshake. Exceptions will get thrown out of RatchetPayload.processPayload(I2PAppContext, RatchetPayload.PayloadCallback, byte[], int, int, boolean) and prevent processing of succeeding blocks.
