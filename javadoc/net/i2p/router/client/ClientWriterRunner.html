    Runnable
    class ClientWriterRunnerextends Object implements Runnable
    Writes I2CP messages to a client asynchronously, so a hung client app cannot take down the whole router. Without this, the JobQueue would block until the client reads from their i2cp socket.

    For external I2CP connections only.
