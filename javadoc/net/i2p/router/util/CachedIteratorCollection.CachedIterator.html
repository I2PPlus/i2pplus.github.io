    Static inner CachedIterator class - implements hasNext(), next() & remove()

    Static to avoid holding an implicit this$0 reference to the enclosing collection, which would prevent GC of the collection (and its owning PeerState) when held in another thread's ThreadLocal after CachedIteratorCollection.clear() is called.
