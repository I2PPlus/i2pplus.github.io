        Simple format, only what stats.i2p needs:
         average

      lastEventSaturation;lastSaturationLimit;extremeEventSaturation;extremeSaturationLimit;
 event count;

      avg freq;exteremeEventCount;lifetimeEventCount;
        Parameters:
        rate - the rate to render
        fudgeQuantity - true to replace the event counts with the placeholder 555, hiding absolute quantities
        Returns:
        the semicolon-separated statistics fields
