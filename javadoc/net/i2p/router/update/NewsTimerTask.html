    Runnable
    class NewsTimerTaskextends SimpleTimer2.TimedEvent
    Task to periodically look for updates to the news.xml, and to keep track of whether that has an announcement for a new version. Also looks for unsigned updates.

    Runs forever on instantiation, can't be stopped.
