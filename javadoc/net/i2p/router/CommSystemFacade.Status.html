    public static enum CommSystemFacade.Status
extends Enum<CommSystemFacade.Status>
    Network connectivity status enumeration for IPv4 and IPv6 transport capabilities. Represents firewall status, NAT configuration, and transport readiness for tunnel participation. Since codes may change.
