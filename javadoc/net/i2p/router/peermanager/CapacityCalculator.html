    class CapacityCalculatorextends Object
    Estimate how many of our tunnels the peer can join per hour.

    Combines measured tunnel accept/reject/fail rates across three time windows (10 min, 1 hour, 1 day) with capability-based penalties and bonuses (congestion caps, unreachable, established connection, XOR distance). Peers with no recent activity (tooOld) default to 1 tunnel/hour. New peers receive a time-based penalty that ramps from PENALTY_NEW down to 0 over the first 2 hours of observed lifetime.

      • estimateCapacity

        private static double estimateCapacity(RateStat acceptStat, RateStat rejectStat, RateStat failedStat, int period)
        Compute a tunnel accept capacity-per-hour for the given period This is perhaps the most critical part of the peer ranking and selection system, so adjust with great care and testing to ensure good network performance and prevent congestion collapse.

        The baseline or "growth factor" is 5. Rejects will not reduce the baseline. Failures will. Let A = accepts, R = rejects, F = fails.

        Parameters:
        acceptStat - Accept counter (1 = 1 accept)
        rejectStat - Reject counter (1 = 1 reject)
        failedStat - Failed counter (100 = 1 fail)
        period - the rate period in ms whose rates are evaluated
        Returns:
        estimated and adjusted accepts per hour, for the given period which is, more or less, max(0, 5 + (A * (A / (A + 2R))) - (4 * F))
