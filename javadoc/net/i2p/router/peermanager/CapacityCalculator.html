net.i2p.router.peermanager

Class CapacityCalculator

  • java.lang.Object
    • net.i2p.router.peermanager.CapacityCalculator

  • class CapacityCalculator
extends Object
    Estimate how many of our tunnels the peer can join per hour. Combines measured tunnel accept/reject/fail rates across three time windows (10 min, 1 hour, 1 day) with capability-based penalties and bonuses (congestion caps, unreachable, established connection, XOR distance). Peers with no recent activity (tooOld) default to 1 tunnel/hour. New peers receive a time-based penalty that ramps from PENALTY_NEW down to 0 over the first 2 hours of observed lifetime.
