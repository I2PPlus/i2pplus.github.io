    public class PayloadGarlicConfigextends GarlicConfig
    A GarlicConfig that holds a single I2NPMessage payload with no sub-cloves.

    This is the leaf node in the garlic clove tree. Every clove in an outbound GarlicMessage is a PayloadGarlicConfig wrapping one I2NPMessage (DataMessage, DeliveryStatusMessage, DatabaseStoreMessage, etc.) along with its delivery instructions.

    Also used directly by netdb MessageWrapper and tunnel TestJob for single-clove garlic wrapping.
