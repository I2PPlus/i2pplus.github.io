      • DEFAULT_MESSAGES_PER_TUNNEL_ESTIMATE

        public static final int DEFAULT_MESSAGES_PER_TUNNEL_ESTIMATE
        This is the estimated number of 1 KB tunnel messages that we'll see in 10 minute lifetime of an exploratory tunnel. We use it as a baseline minimum for estimating tunnel bandwidth, if accepted.

        600 KB in 10 minutes equals ~4 KBps - increased from 200 for better bandwidth allocation per transit tunnel.
