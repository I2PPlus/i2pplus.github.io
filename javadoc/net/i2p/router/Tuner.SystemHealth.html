    • Field Details

      • MIN_FACTOR_SCORE

        static final double MIN_FACTOR_SCORE
        Floor applied to each factor before the composite is computed.

        The composite is a weighted geometric mean, so its factors multiply: one factor that saturates at 0.0 drives the product — and therefore the health shown on the tuning page — to exactly 0% no matter how healthy the rest of the router is. That 0% is also below BaseParam.DEGRADED_THRESHOLD, so a score pinned there silently freezes every auto-tuned param at its factory default, which makes the page read "Degraded (0%)" and stops tuning without any hint why. Flooring keeps one saturated factor worth only its own weight share while the all-bad worst case still sits well under the degraded threshold, so the safety behaviour is preserved.

      • compositeScore

        static double compositeScore(double[] factors, double[] weights)
        Weighted geometric mean of the available factors.

        NaN factors mean "insufficient data" and are skipped along with their weights, so the remaining weights are renormalized to 1.0. Every participating factor is floored at MIN_FACTOR_SCORE first — without that floor a single saturated factor zeroes the aggregate and the score stops discriminating between bad and catastrophic.

        Parameters:
        factors - per-factor scores in [0,1]; NaN for no data
        weights - matching per-factor weights, summing to 1.0
        Returns:
        the composite score in [0,1], or 1.0 if no factor has data
