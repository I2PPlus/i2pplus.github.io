    net.i2p.router.transport.udp.EstablishmentManager
    public class EstablishmentManagerextends Object
    Coordinate the establishment of new sessions - both inbound and outbound. This has its own thread to add packets to the packet queue when necessary, as well as to drop any failed establishment attempts.

      • MAX_OB_ESTABLISH_TIME

        static final AtomicLong MAX_OB_ESTABLISH_TIME
        Kill any outbound that takes more than this. Two round trips (Req-Created-Confirmed-Data) for direct; 3 1/2 round trips (RReq-RResp+Intro-HolePunch-Req-Created-Confirmed-Data) for indirect. Tuned to ~3-5x average establish time. The Tuner further adjusts this at runtime.

        static final AtomicLong MAX_IB_ESTABLISH_TIME
        Kill any inbound that takes more than this. One round trip (Created-Confirmed), possibly two for SSU2 with retry. Tuned to ~3-5x average establish time; the Tuner further adjusts this at runtime.

      • CORRUPT_CONFIRM_LOG_INTERVAL_MS

        static final long CORRUPT_CONFIRM_LOG_INTERVAL_MS
        Minimum gap between "Corrupt SessionConfirmed" reports.

        This one warning was 9.2% of an entire log - roughly 180 lines a minute - and it carries no reason, so the volume bought no information. Two causes are folded into this one catch: a peer that legitimately failed the handshake and then sent SessionConfirmed anyway, which throws IllegalStateException on an already-FAILED state and is ordinary race behaviour; and a genuine key or payload rejection. They need opposite responses, so the reason is now reported and repeats are counted.

      • SLOW_ESTABLISH_MS

        static final long SLOW_ESTABLISH_MS
        Completion time above which a handshake counts as slow.

        The recorded udp.*EstablishTime stats only expose a mean, and the mean is useless for sizing the deadline: measured live, successful outbound handshakes average 196ms, so 4x the mean targets 784ms - below the 2250 that abandoned 117 handshakes a minute. Latency here is heavily right-skewed, and the abandonments came from the tail, not the body. This threshold marks the tail so a tuner has something to read. It sits a quarter of the validated floor, so a healthy link records none.

      • inboundEstablishBudget

        static long inboundEstablishBudget(boolean isRetrySent, long maxEstablishTime, long retrySentMaxTime)
        The deadline an inbound attempt was actually given.

        This was written as a disjunction - lifetime > base || (retried && lifetime >= retryBudget) - which never extended anything. The first clause expires the attempt at the base on its own, so the second could only matter when the retry budget was the smaller of the two, and then it made a retried attempt die sooner. Observed live: with a 4000ms base and a 3750ms retry budget, a retried attempt died at 3751ms.

        The predicate now asks this method for the deadline, so the two cannot disagree and the retry budget means what IB_RETRY_SENT_MAX_TIME exists for: five retransmit delays of extra patience for an attempt that has already tried again.

        Parameters:
        isRetrySent - true if the attempt had already sent a retry
        maxEstablishTime - the base deadline
        retrySentMaxTime - the extended budget available after a retry
        Returns:
        the effective deadline in milliseconds

        void receiveSessionDestroy(RemoteHostId from)
        Got a SessionDestroy - maybe during an inbound establish?

        A SessionDestroy received before a session exists is only informational: there is no state to drop, and a peer that never completed an establishment has nothing to tear down.
