    public static interface SSU2Payload.PayloadCallback
    For all callbacks, recommend throwing exceptions only from the handshake. Exceptions will get thrown out of SSU2Payload.processPayload(I2PAppContext, SSU2Payload.PayloadCallback, byte[], int, int, boolean, RemoteHostId) and prevent processing of succeeding blocks.
