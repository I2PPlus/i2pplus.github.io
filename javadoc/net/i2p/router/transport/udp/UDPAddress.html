net.i2p.router.transport.udp

Class UDPAddress

  • java.lang.Object
    • net.i2p.router.transport.udp.UDPAddress

  • class UDPAddress
extends Object
    Parses and caches the host, port, introducer list, MTU, and key data from a RouterAddress for the SSU and SSU2 transport styles.
