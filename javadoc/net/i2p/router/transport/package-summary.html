    package net.i2p.router.transport

    Transport framework: the transport lifecycle and pool management, connection handling and bandwidth throttling for the transports (NTCP2 over TCP, SSU over UDP) that carry I2P messages between routers, plus NAT traversal via UPnP and GeoIP country lookups. Not for use by apps, clients or plugins.
