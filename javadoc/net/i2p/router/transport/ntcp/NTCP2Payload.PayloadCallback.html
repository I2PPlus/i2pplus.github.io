    public static interface NTCP2Payload.PayloadCallback
    For all callbacks, recommend throwing exceptions only from the handshake. Exceptions will get thrown out of NTCP2Payload.processPayload(I2PAppContext, NTCP2Payload.PayloadCallback, byte[], int, int, boolean) and prevent processing of succeeding blocks.
