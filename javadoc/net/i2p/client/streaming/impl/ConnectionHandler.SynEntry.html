    Accept-queue bookkeeping for one enqueued SYN.

    Holds the enqueue clock-time and the accept-timeout snapshot taken at that moment. The timeout is snapshotted because the adaptive ConnectionHandler.getEffectiveAcceptTimeout() moves as tunnel health changes; the expiry must match what the client was quoted, not what health looks like later. A worker that polls the SYN stores a fresh record, so expiry keeps tracking the most recent quote.
