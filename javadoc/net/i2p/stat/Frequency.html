    net.i2p.stat.Frequency
    public class Frequencyextends Object
    Manage the calculation of a moving average event frequency over a certain period.

    This provides lifetime, and rolling average, frequency counts. Unlike Rate, it does not support "bucketed" averages. There is no tracking of the event frequency in the current or last bucket. There are no buckets at all.

    Depending on what you want, a rolling average might be better than buckets. Or not.
