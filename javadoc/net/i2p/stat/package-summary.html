Search page
    package net.i2p.stat

    Statistics collection framework used throughout the router for adaptive operation, monitoring and debugging.

    RateStat and Rate average rates over 5-minute, 60-minute and lifetime periods, Frequency counts events, StatManager is the central registry, and selected statistics persist across restarts.
