    public class SocketTimeoutextends SimpleTimer2.TimedEvent
    Implements one or two timers; one for inactivity, that is reset by resetTimer(), and optionally, a total time since instantiation, that is configured by setTotalTimeoutPeriod(long).

    On timer expiration, this will close a provided socket, and/or run a configured job.

    Deprecated for external use. It is only used by EepGet, its subclasses, and Syndie. Take care not to break Syndie. The only advantage seems to be a total timeout period, which is the second argument to EepGet.fetch(long, long, long), which is most likely always set to -1.

    Not for use by plugins or external applications, subject to change.

    Use socket.setsotimeout instead?
