    net.i2p.data.Base32
    public class Base32extends Object
    Encodes and decodes to and from Base32 notation. Ref: RFC 3548

    Don't bother with '=' padding characters on encode or accept them on decode (i.e. don't require 5-character groups). No whitespace allowed.

    Decode accepts upper or lower case.
