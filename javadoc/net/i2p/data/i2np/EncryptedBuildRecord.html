    public class EncryptedBuildRecordextends SimpleDataStructure
    ElGamal-encrypted request or response. 528 bytes. Previously stored in a ByteArray. May or may not be AES layer-encrypted.

    Note that these are layer-encrypted and layer-decrypted in-place. Do not cache.
