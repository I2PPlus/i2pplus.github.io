        public MailPart(String uidl, AtomicInteger id, Buffer readBuffer, InputStream in, ReadCounter counter, String[] hdrlines) throws IOException
        Probably the same as InputStream but a different interface. were already parsed in Mail. Null otherwise.
        Parameters:
        uidl - the IMAP UID of the owning message, kept in the public uidl field
        id - a counter from which this part takes its next sequence number
        readBuffer - has zero offset for top-level MailPart.
        in - used for reading (NOT readBuffer.getInputStream())
        counter - used for counting how much we have read.
        hdrlines - non-null for top-level MailPart, where they
        Throws:
        IOException - if the header block cannot be read from in
