    java.lang.Object
    org.klomp.snark.web.I2PSnarkConfigure
    class I2PSnarkConfigureextends Object
    Renders the three forms of the /configure page - main settings, torrent creation filters, and tracker management - and applies submitted settings back to SnarkManager. Extracted wholesale from I2PSnarkServlet; bodies are verbatim moves qualified for servlet access via srv.
