    public class BandwidthGraphextends SimpleTimer2.TimedEvent
    Self-contained bandwidth sampler for the snark screen log graph. Every SAMPLE_INTERVAL_MS, snapshots the cumulative traffic counters (peer wire rates summed over torrents, datagram counters fed by DatagramSender and the receive paths, HTTP tracker counters fed by I2PSnarkUtil) and stores per-interval byte totals in a fixed ring. Runs for the webapp's lifetime, independent of any browser being open, so the graph stays continuous. Flushes to the tmp dir every FLUSH_INTERVAL_MS so history survives webapp or router restarts; entries older than WINDOW_MS are dropped on load.
