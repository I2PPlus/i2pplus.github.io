      • countPlottedSeries

        static int countPlottedSeries(List<PlotElement> plotElements)
        Counts the independently plotted series in a graph, which decides whether the lines are drawn solid or dotted.

        Counts Line and Area together. An area counts because it is drawn with an outline, so a graph made of a filled total plus a subset line has two lines on it and the subset must be dotted to match. Stack stays excluded: a stack is one quantity split into bands, not several series.

        Parameters:
        plotElements - the graph's plot elements, may be null
        Returns:
        number of plotted series, zero for an empty or null list
